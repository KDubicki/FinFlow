## 2. The price-return bias, per universe

The MVP is explicitly price return (ADR 0009). Returns understate total return
by the distribution yield, and the size of that gap varies enormously by
universe. This table is why a strategy's universe is part of how its results are
read.

| Universe | Approx. yield drag | What it does to a long-only backtest |
|---|---|---|
| `precious_metals` | ~0% on the metals, 1.5–2% on the miners | Negligible on GLD/IAU/SGOL/SLV — price return *is* total return for a physically backed trust. |
| `equity_core` | 0.6% (QQQ) to 3.0% (EFA) | A modest, systematic understatement. Comparisons within the universe stay honest; comparisons against a total-return index do not. |
| `sectors` | 0.7% (XLK) to 3.5% (XLRE) | Uneven **across** the universe, which is worse than uniform: a cross-sectional rank that ignores it is systematically biased against staples, utilities and real estate. |
| `rates_credit` | 2.5% (SHY) to **6.5% (HYG)** | **Material to the point of invalidating a long-only result.** A price-return backtest of HYG is simply wrong. |
| `broad_commodities` | 0% | No distributions. The bias here is roll yield instead, which *is* in the price series and therefore correctly captured. |
| `cross_asset` | Mixed, 0–6.5% | Mixed is the dangerous case: comparing SPY against HYG on price return silently favours SPY by several percent a year. |

**Consequences already enforced elsewhere:** `rates_credit` strategies ship as
relative or long/short forms, where the carry largely cancels between legs; a
long-only HYG strategy is not shipped at all.

## 3. UCITS mapping verification

`scripts/verify_ucits_mapping.py` measures return correlation, annualized
tracking difference and overlapping history for every `ucits_equivalent` in the
registry.

**Status: measured against synthetic data only; the live measurement is
pending a backfill of the `.uk` lines.**

Run on 2026-09-06 against the synthetic warehouse, every pair came back `UNFIT`
with correlations near zero. That is the **correct** result for that data and
worth recording rather than hiding: the synthetic source seeds each symbol
independently, so GLD and IGLN.UK are uncorrelated by construction. The run
therefore demonstrates that the check has teeth — it would fail exactly as
loudly if a real mapping were wrong.

What the live run has to establish, per pair:

| Measurement | Threshold | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Daily return correlation | ≥ 0.95 | Below it, the UCITS line is a different bet with a similar name, not a substitute. |
| Tracking difference | Reported, not gated | An accumulating share class drifts up against a distributing US line by roughly the dividend yield. That is §6.4's bias showing up as a number, not a defect. |
| Overlapping sessions | ≥ 250 | Under a year of common history, the correlation is an anecdote. |

**Known before measuring:** every UCITS line is younger than its US original,
several by a decade (DTLA.UK from 2015 against TLT from 2002; EIMI.UK from 2014
against EEM from 2003). A research backtest that starts in 2005 cannot be
executed in the live universe, and the live universe's start date is the
binding one.

Nine of the forty-two instruments carry a mapping. The rest — the sectors, the
miners, the commodity funds, IWM, EFA — are **research-only**, stated in the
registry rather than discovered at the broker.
